Description

An excellent opportunity to acquire a well-presented three-bedroom detached residence together with a substantial single-storey commercial premises, ideally suited to a variety of business uses.

The residential accommodation is complemented by a generous commercial unit currently laid out as a florist, together with extensive tarmac parking to the front, rear garden/yard space and excellent side access.

Location

Situated adjacent to the Athy distributor road, Fortbarrington Road is ideally positioned within easy reach of Athy town and its excellent range of local amenities. The property also benefits from convenient access to the M9 (via N78), with the nearby towns of Kildare, Newbridge and Naas all within easy reach. Athy is a picturesque market town set at the meeting point of the Grand Canal and River Barrow. Rich in history and surrounded by attractive countryside, the town offers a relaxed setting with a strong sense of community and an excellent range of local amenities. The weekly farmers` market, historic landmarks and scenic green spaces add to its appeal.

The town caters well for families and active lifestyles, with a selection of schools, sporting clubs and recreational facilities available locally. Athy also benefits from excellent transport connections, with convenient access to the M7 and M9 motorways, regular bus services and the Dublin Waterford railway line. Rail services provide a direct connection to Dublin Heuston, with journey times of under 45 minutes.
